# Privacy Policy — Dentaku

**Last updated: July 2026**
**Developer: Ashley Every / AEDO (aedo.jp)**

## Overview

Dentaku is a precision unit conversion and calculation toolkit. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Data Collection

Dentaku does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app operates entirely on your device with no server communication except:

- **Currency exchange rates** — fetched from ExchangeRate-API to provide live FX conversion. No personal data is sent with this request.

## Data Storage

All app data (preferences, recent conversions, custom units) is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device.

## Third Party Services

Dentaku uses ExchangeRate-API solely to fetch currency exchange rates. No personal information is transmitted to this service.

## Voice Input

Voice recognition is processed entirely on-device by Apple's Speech Recognition framework. No audio data is transmitted to AEDO or any third party.

## Children's Privacy

Dentaku does not collect any data from users of any age.

## Contact

For privacy questions: ash@aedo.jp

## Changes

Any changes to this policy will be reflected in an updated version of this document.
