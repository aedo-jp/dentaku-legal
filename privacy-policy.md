# Privacy Policy — Dentaku

**Last updated:** October 2026
**Effective date:** Upon App Store release

## Who we are

Dentaku is developed and operated by Ash Every Design Office (Ashley Every), based in Yamanashi, Japan.

**Contact:** hello@aedo.jp
**Location:** Yamanashi, Japan

This Privacy Policy explains how Dentaku handles your information when you use our app.

## The short version

- **We do not collect personal information** such as your name, email address, or location.
- **Your data stays on your device.** All preferences, recent conversions, and custom units are stored locally only.
- **Currency rates are fetched anonymously.** No personal data is sent with this request.
- **Voice input uses Apple's speech recognition.** If you use it, your speech may be sent to Apple to be turned into text. We never receive your audio.
- **We do not sell your data.** We never have and never will.
- **We do not show ads.** Dentaku is a one-time purchase with no advertising.

## 1. What data Dentaku processes

### 1a. App preferences and data (stored locally only)

Dentaku stores the following information locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party:

- Your unit system preference (metric/imperial)
- Your selected modes (architecture/trades/cooking)
- Recent unit conversion pairs
- Favourite conversion pairs
- Custom units you have created
- Calculator tool settings and defaults

**Purpose:** To personalise your experience and remember your settings between uses.

### 1b. Currency exchange rates

When you use the FX (currency) conversion feature, Dentaku fetches daily exchange rates from Frankfurter (frankfurter.dev), a free public exchange-rate service. This request:

- Contains no personal information
- Does not include your identity, location, or device identifier
- Is cached on your device for 24 hours to minimise requests
- Is used solely to display current exchange rates

Like any internet request, it necessarily reveals your device's IP address to the service so that it can reply. Frankfurter states that it does not collect or log personal data, IP addresses, or request URLs. Dentaku also includes a built-in set of recent rates, so currency conversion still works if you are offline.

Exchange rates shown in Dentaku are indicative only, are not suitable for trading or financial transactions, and may differ from the rates your bank or card provider uses.

**Purpose:** To provide currency conversion rates.

### 1c. Voice input

Voice input is optional and only starts when you tap the microphone button. It uses Apple's Speech Recognition, which is part of iOS. Depending on your device and settings, your speech may be sent to Apple's servers to be converted into text, and is then handled under Apple's privacy policy. Dentaku does not receive, record, or store your audio. The resulting text is used only to set up your conversion, and is not kept or sent anywhere by Dentaku.

You can turn off microphone and speech recognition access for Dentaku at any time in iOS Settings. The rest of the app works without it.

**Purpose:** To let you speak conversions instead of typing them.

## 2. Data we do not collect

To be explicitly clear, Dentaku does not collect:

- Your name, email address, or contact information

- Browsing history or usage patterns

- Biometric data
- Health or medical information
- Photos or camera data

## 4. Your rights

**All users:**

- You can stop using Dentaku at any time, which ends all data processing
- You can delete the app, which removes all locally stored data from your device
- You can contact us at hello@aedo.jp with any privacy concerns

**EU/EEA users (under GDPR):**

- Right to access the personal data we hold about you
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority

*Note: Because Dentaku does not collect personal data that identifies you, most of these rights have limited practical application.*

**Japan users (under APPI):**

- Right to disclosure of retained personal information
- Right to correction of incorrect personal information

**Australian users (under Privacy Act 1988):**

- Right to access personal information we hold about you
- Right to correct personal information that is inaccurate

**US users (including California residents under CCPA/CPRA):**

- We do not sell your personal information — ever
- We do not use your data for targeted advertising of any kind

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@aedo.jp. We will respond within 30 days.

## 5. Data security

- All communications between Dentaku and the exchange-rate service use HTTPS encryption
- No personal data is stored on our servers — we do not operate servers that hold your data
- All app data is stored in your device's local storage

## 6. Data retention

**App preferences and conversion data:** Stored locally on your device for as long as the app is installed. Deleted when you uninstall the app.

**Currency rate cache:** Stored locally on your device, refreshed every 24 hours, cleared when the app is uninstalled.

We do not maintain any database of your data on our servers.

## 7. Children's privacy

Dentaku is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
